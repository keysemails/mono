# Claim Prose

The "claim" part of a filename should be written as prose - a readable phrase that captures the main content, idea, or concept.

## Why Prose?

When claims are written as prose, they can be:
- Used directly in sentences when referencing
- Understood at a glance without opening the file
- Linked naturally in wiki-style references

## Examples

**Good (prose):**
- `c - an action is a logical unit of work.md`
- `c - commits should happen immediately after changes.md`
- `mech - cant just take spells from found spell books.md`

**Avoid (keywords/titles):**
- `c - action-definition.md`
- `c - commit-timing.md`
- `mech - spell-book-rules.md`

## When Prose Doesn't Fit

For larger resources (sprints, research results), use descriptive names:
- `sprint - terrain research sprint.md`
- `research - terrain brush heightmap mapping.md`
